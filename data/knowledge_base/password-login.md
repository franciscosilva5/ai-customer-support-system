# Password and Login Problems

Customers who forget their password can request a password reset from the NovaDesk login page.

A password-reset email is sent to the email address associated with the account.

If the email does not arrive, customers should:

- check spam or junk folders
- confirm that the correct email address was entered
- wait several minutes before requesting another reset email

Password-reset links expire after 60 minutes.

Customers who no longer have access to their registered email address must contact NovaDesk Support for account recovery.

Support may request additional information to verify account ownership.
