# Account and Workspace Deletion

Users can delete their personal NovaDesk account from:

Settings > Account > Delete Account

Workspace owners can delete a workspace from:

Workspace Settings > General > Delete Workspace

Deletion is permanent after the confirmation process is completed.

Customers should export any information they wish to keep before deletion.

Deleting a paid workspace does not automatically issue a refund.

Requests involving inaccessible accounts or disputed ownership should be escalated to NovaDesk Support.
