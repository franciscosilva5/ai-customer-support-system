# Billing and Subscription Payments

NovaDesk subscriptions are billed monthly or annually depending on the plan selected by the customer.

Customers can view invoices and billing history from:

Settings > Billing > Invoices

NovaDesk accepts major credit and debit cards.

If a payment fails, NovaDesk automatically retries the payment during the following days. The account remains accessible during the initial retry period.

Customers should update expired or invalid payment methods under:

Settings > Billing > Payment Method

Repeated payment failures may cause the workspace subscription to be temporarily suspended.

Customers who believe they were charged incorrectly should contact support with the invoice number and billing date.
