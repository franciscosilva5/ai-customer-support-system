# Canceling a Subscription

Workspace owners can cancel a NovaDesk paid subscription from:

Settings > Billing > Manage Subscription > Cancel Subscription

Cancellation stops the next automatic renewal.

The workspace remains on the paid plan until the end of the current billing period.

After the billing period ends, the workspace is moved to the Free plan.

Canceling a subscription does not automatically generate a refund for previous payments.

Customers who cannot access the billing page should contact NovaDesk Support.
