# Exporting Workspace Data

Workspace owners and administrators can export NovaDesk workspace data.

The export option is available under:

Workspace Settings > Data > Export

Depending on workspace size, preparation of the export file may take several minutes.

When the export is ready, NovaDesk provides a download link.

Export links expire after 24 hours.

If an export repeatedly fails, the customer should contact NovaDesk Support and provide the workspace name and approximate time of the failed export.
