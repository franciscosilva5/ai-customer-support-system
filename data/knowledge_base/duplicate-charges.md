# Duplicate Charges

A customer may occasionally see what appears to be more than one NovaDesk charge.

Before requesting a refund, customers should check whether:

- one charge is a temporary card authorization
- multiple NovaDesk workspaces use the same payment method
- an annual renewal occurred near another purchase

Temporary card authorizations normally disappear automatically according to the customer's bank processing time.

If two completed charges exist for the same workspace, subscription, and billing period, the customer should contact NovaDesk Support.

Support should request:

- account email
- workspace name
- invoice numbers
- billing dates

Confirmed duplicate charges are eligible for review and refund.
