# Refund Policy

NovaDesk customers may request a refund within 14 days of their first subscription purchase.

Renewal payments are generally non-refundable once the new billing period has started.

Exceptions may be reviewed when there is evidence of:

- duplicate billing
- a confirmed technical billing error
- an unauthorized transaction

Refund requests must be submitted to NovaDesk Support.

A refund review does not guarantee approval.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing time depends on the customer's payment provider and bank.
